About the project

DreamScape is an Android app that turns dream journaling into a living, visual, and repeatable practice.

Each recorded dream becomes a star on a personal Star Map. Users can name dreams, tag them, set their date and importance, and revisit them over time through a spaced-repetition-inspired recall system called Memento.

Every successful recall strengthens the dream: it gains a level, adds an inhabitant, unlocks or powers buildings, and activates Boons that represent the emotional energy of that dream.

DreamScape combines dream journaling, memory practice, personal symbolism, and cozy gamification. Its goal is to help users keep a panoramic view of their dream life, return to meaningful dreams over time, and transform private dream notes into an evolving constellation of memory and meaning.
